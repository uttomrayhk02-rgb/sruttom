# Marketing Analysis Framework

A repeatable system for moving from observations to defensible marketing decisions.

## Analysis layers

```text
1. Business
   ↓
2. Customer + Intent
   ↓
3. Offer
   ↓
4. Acquisition
   ↓
5. Landing Page
   ↓
6. Tracking
   ↓
7. Performance
   ↓
8. Conversion
   ↓
9. Economics
```

## Root-cause analysis

When performance changes, investigate in this order:

1. **Data quality** — Is tracking trustworthy?
2. **Demand** — Did search volume / intent change?
3. **Traffic quality** — Are the right users arriving?
4. **Message / offer** — Does the page and ad match intent?
5. **Conversion friction** — What prevents action?
6. **Sales quality** — Are leads becoming real opportunities?
7. **Economics** — Does the unit economics make sense?

## Decision format

**Observation:** What the data shows.

**Evidence:** Which segment or metric supports it.

**Hypothesis:** Why it may be happening.

**Validation:** What would confirm or reject the hypothesis.

**Action:** What should change.

**Measurement:** How success will be evaluated.

## Analysis rule

Avoid conclusions such as “CTR is low, therefore the ads are bad.”

Instead ask:

> **Compared with what, for which segment, under which conditions, and what business outcome changed?**
