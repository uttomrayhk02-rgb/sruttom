## Experiment template

### Hypothesis
What do I believe will happen?

### Problem
What evidence suggests this is worth testing?

### Change
What exactly will change?

### Measurement
Which KPI will determine success?

### Result
What happened?

### Learning
What did I learn?

### Next action
What should be tested or changed next?
