# Google Ads Audit System

A practical, repeatable framework for diagnosing Google Ads performance and turning findings into prioritized actions.

## Audit flow

```text
Account
  ↓
Campaign Structure
  ↓
Keywords + Search Terms
  ↓
Ads + Assets
  ↓
Bidding + Budget
  ↓
Conversions + Tracking
  ↓
Landing Page
  ↓
Business Economics
  ↓
Prioritized Actions
```

## Core checks

- Campaign and ad-group structure
- Search intent and keyword-to-landing-page alignment
- Match-type strategy
- Search-term quality and negative keywords
- Ad relevance and messaging
- Location and schedule settings
- Budget allocation
- Bid strategy and learning status
- Primary vs secondary conversions
- GTM / GA4 / Google Ads tracking consistency
- Landing-page conversion friction
- Lead quality and business outcomes

## Prioritization model

| Priority | Meaning |
|---|---|
| P0 | Tracking or measurement failure |
| P1 | Major spend / lead-quality risk |
| P2 | Material optimization opportunity |
| P3 | Testing / refinement |

## Deliverable

Every audit should end with:

1. Finding
2. Evidence
3. Likely cause
4. Business impact
5. Recommended action
6. Priority
7. Measurement plan

> This is a framework for analysis, not a promise of campaign performance.
